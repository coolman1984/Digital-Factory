Present

A guided look for a visitor: assembled, taken apart, seen through, cut open, the head at work, and back together.

Take apart

Each assembly moves out along its own path, in order; back to 0 it returns exactly to its place. Key: E.

See inside

X-ray turns the covers to glass outlines (key X). Cut open slices the covers with a movable section; the red faces are the cut (key C). The front feeder trolley is wheeled away while the machine is cut. Run the head plays a pick-and-place cycle (Space).

Camera

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a part to select it. Keys: arrows orbit, + and − zoom, 0 resets, Esc clears the selection.

    About this model

    An illustrative educational reconstruction of a generic gantry pick-and-place machine, built from code in Blender (tools/blender/mounter.py): 40 named parts in 14 assemblies. It is not a vendor's machine and is not for maintenance or safety work. Plant EG-01 is fictional.
