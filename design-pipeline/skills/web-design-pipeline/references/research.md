# Stage 2 · Researching award-level references (free)

Goal: 4–6 references that teach a transferable principle for THIS subject. Not a mood board of pretty sites.

## Where to look (all free)
- **Taste-skill's study**: `$S/design-taste-frontend/references/research.md` (§10 lists the 25 highest-scored sites and
  their ideas) and `library/sites.json` (247 gallery sites with sector, idea and composition). Search it first, offline.
- **Live galleries** through the agent's web search and fetch tools: Awwwards (Sites of the Day, Honorable Mentions),
  CSS Design Awards, One Page Love, SiteInspire, Godly, Land-book, The FWA. For app screens and dashboards: published
  product sites of category leaders and their documentation screenshots.
- **The subject's own world**: the client's sector leaders, its city, its trade's printed matter (labels, signs,
  timetables). These give the idea; galleries give the craft.

## Query recipe
`"<sector> website" site:awwwards.com`, `"<sector>" sites of the day`, `"<subject noun> landing page" design award`,
`"<product type> dashboard" UI` for app screens. Use two or three queries, not twenty.

## What to write in .design-pipeline/REFERENCES.md

| # | Site (URL) | Why it is good (one line) | Principle to transfer | Not to copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | artwork, copy, logo, claims |

Rules:
- At least one reference from outside the client's category (the strongest ideas usually transfer across sectors).
- State the principle as a rule you can apply ("one oversized number carries the hero", "the timetable is the layout").
- Never copy artwork, photographs, copy, logos or numbers; transfer grammar only.
- If research is unavailable (offline), say so and rely on the taste study.
