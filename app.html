      Nine agents watch Plant EG-01 and its offices. Each one says what it sees with the numbers behind it, forms options, simulates every option on a two-hour forecast of the six main lines and everything that feeds them, then recommends one. It acts on its own only on Auto and only when the step can be undone; on Approve a person decides, and a cheap reversible step goes ahead after two minutes unless someone vetoes it. Every step gets an audit ID.
