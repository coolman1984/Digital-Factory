# Product: Digital Factory

**One line.** A live digital twin, AI agent crew and production copilot for an electronics plant, shown to the head of production at Plant EG-01 (a fictional TV plant with six main assembly lines) as proof that the plant can be AI-driven ahead of the industry's 2030 goal.

**Audience.** One senior production leader and his managers. Engineers at heart, short on time, sceptical of slideware. They judge by: is it real, is it about *my* line, can I act on it.

**Surfaces and modes (impeccable).**
| Surface | File | Mode | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website | index.html | Persuade | Make the vision feel inevitable and already built |
| Presentation | deck.html | Persuade / Read | Tell the case in the meeting, live demo inside |
| Command room | app.html | Operate | Let him run Plant EG-01 (the same model as the 3D plant): decide the agents' decisions with the options they simulated, alarms, control previewed on the forecast, what-if, time machine |
| Live plant | plant.html | Operate / Experience | Let him walk a whole TV plant in 3D, follow one TV, see SAP, GMES, S&OP, MRP and every department on the same numbers, and take the decision when a cause travels to an order |

**Pinned (brand).** Brand UI kit in `assets/kit/`: brand blue, Inter, rounded geometry, light and dark. No third-party artwork or logos; no real company or city named for the plant. All plant data simulated and labelled as such. Every number on the site is sourced or marked as sample.

**Tone.** Plain, confident, world-class English without the word "world-class". Short sentences. Verbs. Numbers with sources.

**Subject inventory (things only this product has).**
1. The production line itself: SMD boards, LCM modules and moulded parts → main line → test → aging → pack, left to right.
2. The unit (a board, a panel module, a TV) moving station to station.
3. Takt time: a beat every few seconds.
4. The andon light: green, amber, red.
5. The engineering drawing: grid, dimension lines, station callouts.
6. The instrument reticle and the live readout (OEE, units, risk).
7. The alarm flood that collapses into one cause.
8. The shift clock and the plan line.
9. The bearing spectrum of a mounter before it fails.
10. The 2030 mandate as a date on the horizon.
