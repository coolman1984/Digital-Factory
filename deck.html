Use case 1 · Predictive maintenance

Fix it on our schedule — not when it breaks.

Without AI · reactive
Running💥 4.5 h unplanned stop
Bearing seizes mid-shift. Line down, parts expedited, overtime to recover.
With Digital Factory · predictive
Running⚠ AI alertRunning · parts reserved25m
AI warns ≈ 40 h ahead. Repair slotted into the shift change. Nobody notices.
  • Vibration, temperature & motor current on every critical machine
  • AI learns each machine's normal signature and predicts remaining useful life
  • Auto-creates the work order, reserves the spare, assigns the technician
  • Industry studies: 30–50% less unplanned downtime, 20–40% longer machine life
Benchmarks: McKinsey & Company research on predictive maintenance; results vary with baseline.

Use case 2 · AI vision quality

Every board. Every component. Every time.

100%

Inspection coverage

Deep-learning models on edge GPUs inspect every unit at line speed — no sampling, no fatigue, no night-shift dip.

≈ 12 ms

Per decision

Bridges, missing parts, tombstones, misalignment, cosmetic scratches — classified with confidence scores.

Root cause

Not just detection

Defects are correlated with process data — e.g. “bridges up 4×, printer pressure drifted” — and fixed at the source.
