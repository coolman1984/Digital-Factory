How it works

Four layers. One clear picture of the whole plant.

Nothing is replaced. Digital Factory sits on top of the machines, MES and ERP the plant already runs.

1 · ConnectEvery machine signal, live
2 · SeeA live digital twin
3 · PredictAI that sees problems coming
4 · ActClear decisions, ranked by value
1 · CONNECT

Every signal, in real time.

PLCs, SMT equipment, AOI and SPI, testers, energy meters, AGVs and the MES stream into one place through open industrial standards. We start read-only, so production is never touched.

2 · SEE

A live digital twin of the plant.

Every line, station and unit, updated every second. One shared picture for operators, engineers and management, on screens and on mobile.

3 · PREDICT

AI that sees problems coming.

Machine-health models forecast failures days ahead. Vision AI inspects every board. Root-cause models link each defect to the process step that caused it.

4 · ACT

Decisions, not dashboards.

Actions ranked by their value in EGP, work orders raised automatically, and a copilot that answers questions about the plant in plain language.
